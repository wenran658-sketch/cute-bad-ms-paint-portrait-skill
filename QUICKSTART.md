# Quick Start

## Basic

Upload a portrait and use:

> Use the Cute Bad MS-Paint Portrait skill. Keep the original hairstyle.

## Couple avatar

> Use the Cute Bad MS-Paint Portrait skill. Keep both hairstyles unchanged, preserve our pose, use a white background, and add only three tiny heart/star doodles.

## More ridiculous

> Use the Cute Bad MS-Paint Portrait skill. Make the drawing quality 30% worse, more jagged and pixelated, but keep our faces cute and recognizable.

## Cleaner avatar

> Use the Cute Bad MS-Paint Portrait skill. Keep the rough mouse-drawn style, but reduce background doodles and make the composition suitable for a profile picture.

## Clothing variation

> Use the Cute Bad MS-Paint Portrait skill. Keep the hair and faces unchanged, but simplify our clothes into matching dark T-shirts.
